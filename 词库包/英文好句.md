# 英语好句
## 一、励志哲理篇（40句）

1. Where there is a will, there is a way.
   有志者，事竟成。

2. Never say die.
   永不言败。

3. No pain, no gain.
   不劳无获。

4. Nothing is impossible to a willing heart.
   心之所愿，无事不成。

5. The best way to predict the future is to create it.
   预测未来的最好方法就是创造未来。

6. Success is not final, failure is not fatal: it is the courage to continue that counts.
   成功不是终点，失败也非末日：最重要的是继续前进的勇气。

7. It does not matter how slowly you go as long as you do not stop.
   只要不停下脚步，走得慢也没关系。

8. Fall seven times, stand up eight.
   跌倒七次，站起来八次。

9. The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.
   千里之行，始于足下。

10. Actions speak louder than words.
    行动胜于言语。

11. Great minds think alike.
    英雄所见略同。

12. When the going gets tough, the tough get going.
    艰难之路，唯勇者行。

13. Do not put off until tomorrow what you can do today.
    今日事，今日毕。

14. If you want something you've never had, you must be willing to do something you've never done.
    想要得到从未拥有过的东西，就要愿意去做从未做过的事。

15. Life is ten percent what happens to you and ninety percent how you respond to it.
    生活是百分之十发生在你身上的事，加上百分之九十你对它的回应。

16. The harder you work for something, the greater you'll feel when you achieve it.
    你为一件事付出的努力越多，实现时的成就感就越大。

17. Don't wait for the perfect moment. Take the moment and make it perfect.
    不要等待完美的时刻，抓住当下，让它变得完美。

18. Dream big and dare to fail.
    志存高远，敢于失败。

19. You miss 100% of the shots you don't take.
    不出手，就永远进不了球。

20. Believe you can and you're halfway there.
    相信自己能做到，你就已经成功了一半。

21. Every accomplishment starts with the decision to try.
    每一份成就，都始于尝试的决定。

22. Little by little, one travels far.
    积跬步，方能至千里。

23. A river cuts through rock, not because of its power, but because of its persistence.
    水滴石穿，不是因其力量，而是因其坚持不懈。

24. The secret of getting ahead is getting started.
    脱颖而出的秘诀就是开始行动。

25. Difficult roads often lead to beautiful destinations.
    艰难的道路，往往通往美丽的终点。

26. Stars can't shine without darkness.
    没有黑暗，星星就无法闪耀。

27. Your limitation—it's only your imagination.
    你的极限，只存在于你的想象之中。

28. Push yourself, because no one else is going to do it for you.
    鞭策自己吧，没人会替你努力。

29. Great things never come from comfort zones.
    伟大的成就，从不来自舒适区。

30. Don't stop when you're tired. Stop when you're done.
    累了不要停，完成了才停。

31. Wake up with determination. Go to bed with satisfaction.
    带着决心醒来，带着满足入睡。

32. Do something today that your future self will thank you for.
    今天做一件事，让未来的自己感谢现在的你。

33. It's going to be hard, but hard does not mean impossible.
    这会很难，但难并不意味着不可能。

34. Sometimes we're tested not to show our weaknesses, but to discover our strengths.
    有时考验我们，不是为了暴露弱点，而是为了发现力量。

35. The key to success is to focus on goals, not obstacles.
    成功的关键在于专注于目标，而非障碍。

36. Either you run the day or the day runs you.
    要么你驾驭这一天，要么这一天驾驭你。

37. I never dreamed about success. I worked for it.
    我从不幻想成功，我只为成功而努力。

38. Try not to become a man of success, but rather try to become a man of value.
    不要试图做成功的人，而要努力做一个有价值的人。

39. In the middle of difficulty lies opportunity.
    困难之中，蕴藏机遇。

40. Life isn't about waiting for the storm to pass, it's about learning to dance in the rain.
    生活不是等待风暴过去，而是学会在雨中翩翩起舞。

## 二、名言警句篇（40句）

41. To be, or not to be, that is the question.
    生存还是毁灭，这是一个问题。—— 莎士比亚

42. Knowledge is power.
    知识就是力量。—— 培根

43. Genius is one percent inspiration and ninety-nine percent perspiration.
    天才是百分之一的灵感加上百分之九十九的汗水。—— 爱迪生

44. I think, therefore I am.
    我思故我在。—— 笛卡尔

45. That which does not kill us makes us stronger.
    凡不能毁灭我的，必使我强大。—— 尼采

46. Imagination is more important than knowledge.
    想象力比知识更重要。—— 爱因斯坦

47. The unexamined life is not worth living.
    未经审视的人生不值得过。—— 苏格拉底

48. I have a dream that one day this nation will rise up and live out the true meaning of its creed.
    我有一个梦想，有一天这个国家会站起来，实现其信条的真正含义。—— 马丁·路德·金

49. Ask not what your country can do for you, ask what you can do for your country.
    不要问国家能为你做什么，要问你能为国家做什么。—— 肯尼迪

50. Be the change you wish to see in the world.
    欲变世界，先变其身。—— 甘地

51. Stay hungry, stay foolish.
    求知若饥，虚心若愚。—— 乔布斯

52. The only thing we have to fear is fear itself.
    我们唯一需要害怕的，就是害怕本身。—— 罗斯福

53. Life is like a box of chocolates. You never know what you're gonna get.
    生活就像一盒巧克力，你永远不知道下一颗是什么味道。—— 《阿甘正传》

54. To err is human, to forgive divine.
    犯错是人之常情，宽恕是神之圣行。—— 蒲柏

55. All that glitters is not gold.
    闪光的不都是金子。—— 莎士比亚

56. Reading makes a full man, conference a ready man, and writing an exact man.
    读书使人充实，讨论使人机智，写作使人严谨。—— 培根

57. Music is the universal language of mankind.
    音乐是人类共同的语言。—— 朗费罗

58. A little learning is a dangerous thing.
    一知半解是危险的。—— 蒲柏

59. The course of true love never did run smooth.
    真爱之路，从不平坦。—— 莎士比亚

60. What's in a name? That which we call a rose by any other name would smell as sweet.
    名字有什么意义？玫瑰不叫玫瑰，依然芬芳如故。—— 莎士比亚

61. Hope is a good breakfast, but it is a bad supper.
    希望是美好的早餐，却是糟糕的晚餐。—— 培根

62. Some books are to be tasted, others to be swallowed, and some few to be chewed and digested.
    书有可浅尝者，有可吞食者，少数则须细嚼慢咽、消化吸收。—— 培根

63. The man who has made up his mind to win will never say "impossible".
    下定决心要赢的人，从来不会说"不可能"。—— 拿破仑

64. Victory belongs to the most persevering.
    胜利属于最坚忍不拔的人。—— 拿破仑

65. Do not go where the path may lead, go instead where there is no path and leave a trail.
    不要走别人已踏平的路，去走无人之路，留下自己的足迹。—— 爱默生

66. The only limit to our realization of tomorrow will be our doubts of today.
    明天的唯一限制，是今天的怀疑。—— 罗斯福

67. Live as if you were to die tomorrow. Learn as if you were to live forever.
    像明天就要死去那样生活，像永远不必死去那样学习。—— 甘地

68. Two roads diverged in a wood, and I took the one less traveled by, and that has made all the difference.
    林中分出两条路，我选了人迹更少的一条，从此决定了我一生的道路。—— 弗罗斯特

69. Keep your face always toward the sunshine, and shadows will fall behind you.
    永远面朝阳光，阴影就会落在你身后。—— 惠特曼

70. We are all in the gutter, but some of us are looking at the stars.
    我们都身处阴沟，但仍有人仰望星空。—— 王尔德

71. Be yourself; everyone else is already taken.
    做你自己，因为别人都已经有人做了。—— 王尔德

72. The truth is rarely pure and never simple.
    真相很少纯粹，也绝不简单。—— 王尔德

73. A room without books is like a body without a soul.
    没有书的房间，如同没有灵魂的躯体。—— 西塞罗

74. While there is life, there is hope.
    一息尚存，希望不灭。

75. Time is money.
    时间就是金钱。—— 富兰克林

76. Early to bed and early to rise makes a man healthy, wealthy and wise.
    早睡早起使人健康、富有、聪明。—— 富兰克林

77. In this world nothing can be said to be certain, except death and taxes.
    世上唯有死亡与税收是确定无疑的。—— 富兰克林

78. Give me liberty, or give me death!
    不自由，毋宁死！—— 亨利

79. My philosophy is that worrying means you suffer twice.
    我的人生哲学是：忧愁意味着你要受两遍苦。—— 《神奇动物在哪里》

80. Not all those who wander are lost.
    并非所有流浪者都迷失了方向。—— 《魔戒》

## 五、学习成长篇（30句）

161. Live and learn.
     活到老，学到老。

162. There is no royal road to learning.
     学无坦途。

163. Learning is a treasure that will follow its owner everywhere.
     学问是随身携带的宝藏。

164. The more you learn, the more you earn.
     学得越多，赚得越多。

165. Knowledge comes from experience; information comes from books.
     知识源于经历，信息来自书本。

166. A book is a dream that you hold in your hand.
     书是握在手里的梦想。

167. Reading is to the mind what exercise is to the body.
     阅读之于头脑，如同锻炼之于身体。

168. Today a reader, tomorrow a leader.
     今天的读者，明天的领袖。

169. The expert in anything was once a beginner.
     任何领域的专家都曾是初学者。

170. Mistakes are proof that you're trying.
     犯错恰恰证明你在努力。

171. You don't have to be great to start, but you have to start to be great.
     开始不需要很优秀，但要优秀必须先开始。

172. Practice like you've never won. Perform like you've never lost.
     像从未赢过一样练习，像从未输过一样表现。

173. Learning without thinking is labor lost; thinking without learning is perilous.
     学而不思则罔，思而不学则殆。

174. Study hard, for the well is deep, and our brains are shallow.
     努力学习吧，井很深，我们的头脑却很浅。

175. The beautiful thing about learning is that no one can take it away from you.
     学习之美在于：没人能把它从你身边夺走。

176. First think, then speak.
     先思后言。

177. Learn from yesterday, live for today, hope for tomorrow.
     从昨天学习，为今天而活，对明天怀抱希望。

178. Genius is nothing but labor and diligence.
     天才不过是勤劳和勤奋。

179. If a thing is worth doing, it is worth doing well.
     值得做的事，就值得做好。

180. Step by step the ladder is ascended.
     拾级而上，梯可登顶。

181. A lazy youth, a lousy age.
     少时懒惰，老来凄凉。

182. No man is his craft's master the first day.
     没有人能一天精通一门手艺。

183. Repetition is the mother of learning.
     重复是学习之母。

184. The roots of education are bitter, but the fruit is sweet.
     教育之根苦涩，其果却甜美。

185. He who asks is a fool for five minutes, but he who does not ask remains a fool forever.
     提问者只是五分钟的傻瓜，不提问者是一辈子的傻瓜。

186. Curiosity is the wick in the candle of learning.
     好奇心是学习之烛的烛芯。

187. Don't learn safety rules simply by rote; rather, learn the reasons behind them.
     不要死记安全规则，要理解规则背后的道理。

188. An investment in knowledge pays the best interest.
     投资知识，收益最佳。—— 富兰克林

189. Tell me and I forget. Teach me and I remember. Involve me and I learn.
     告诉我，我会忘记；教给我，我会记住；让我参与，我才能学会。

190. What we learn with pleasure we never forget.
     快乐学来的东西，永远不会忘记。

## 六、情感友谊篇（35句）

191. True friendship is a plant of slow growth.
     真正的友谊是慢慢生长的植物。

192. A real friend is one who walks in when the rest of the world walks out.
     真正的朋友，是全世界都离你而去时，走进来的人。

193. Friendship is a sheltering tree.
     友谊是一棵遮风挡雨的树。

194. The language of friendship is not words but meanings.
     友谊的语言不在言辞，而在心意。

195. A single rose can be my garden; a single friend, my world.
     一朵玫瑰可以是我的花园，一个朋友可以是我的世界。

196. To love and be loved is to feel the sun from both sides.
     爱人与被爱，如同感受两面照耀来的阳光。

197. Love is composed of a single soul inhabiting two bodies.
     爱是一个灵魂栖息在两个身体里。

198. The best thing to hold onto in life is each other.
     人生中最值得紧握的，是彼此的手。

199. Being deeply loved by someone gives you strength, while loving someone deeply gives you courage.
     被人深爱给你力量，深爱他人给你勇气。

200. Where love is concerned, too much is not even enough.
     谈到爱，再多也不嫌多。

201. Love is not about possession; it's about appreciation.
     爱不是占有，而是欣赏。

202. To the world you may be one person, but to one person you may be the world.
     对世界而言你是一个人，但对某个人而言，你可能是整个世界。

203. Distance doesn't separate people; silence does.
     分开人的不是距离，而是沉默。

204. Family is not an important thing. It's everything.
     家庭不是重要的事，而是一切。

205. The love of a family is life's greatest blessing.
     家人的爱是人生最大的福气。

206. A mother's arms are made of tenderness; children sleep soundly in them.
     母亲的怀抱温柔似水，孩子安睡其中。

207. Home is the nicest word there is.
     "家"是世上最温暖的词。

208. Gratitude turns what we have into enough.
     感恩让我们所拥有的变得足够。

209. Forgiveness is the fragrance that the violet sheds on the heel that has crushed it.
     宽恕是紫罗兰被踩碎后，洒在鞋跟上的芬芳。
